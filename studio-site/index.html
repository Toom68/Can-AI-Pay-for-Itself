Questions, answered honestly

Who actually runs this?

A student in Brisbane, with modern AI-assisted tools doing the heavy lifting. That's the whole secret to the price — low overheads, not cut corners. You get a real person on email and a finished site before any money is mentioned.

How can the preview be free?

Because building the site is how we prove we can do the job. We'd rather show you something real than send you a brochure about ourselves. If you don't want it, we've lost an afternoon, not your money.

Do I need my own domain?

If you already own one, we connect it free. If not, we'll help you buy one — roughly A$15–25/year paid to the registrar in your name, so it's yours. Want to start without one? We can launch on a free address first and connect a domain later.

Where is the site hosted? What does hosting cost?

Your site is plain, fast web pages hosted on a free static-web host — so there's no monthly hosting bill from us at all. The only recurring cost is your domain registration (~A$15–25/yr) if you choose to have one.

What if I want changes later?

One round of tweaks is included after delivery. After that you can take the A$15/month care plan (small edits whenever you need them, cancel anytime), ask us for a one-off change, or edit it yourself — you own all the files.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or PayID, after you've seen the finished site and said yes. We'll send a simple invoice. No card payments, no deposits before you've seen the work.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. Once you've paid, the site and its files are yours — we'll hand them over and you're free to host or edit them anywhere. The domain (if you have one) is registered in your name too.

Curious what we'd build for you?

Email us your business name and suburb — if we haven't already built your preview, that's the fastest way to get one started. Free to look at, as always.
