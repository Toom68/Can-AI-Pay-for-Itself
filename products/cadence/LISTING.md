**Title:** Cadence — chiptune music studio (Windows)

**Tagline:** A native, offline sequencer: step sequencing, piano roll, 202
synthesized sounds, WAV & MIDI export. No Electron, no accounts, no cloud.

**Body:**

> Cadence is a focused, multi-track songwriting studio that runs natively on
> your desktop — a real app, not a web page. Write drum patterns in the step
> sequencer, melodies in the piano roll, arrange them into songs across
> multiple instrument tracks, mix in per-track effects, and export your track
> as WAV or MIDI.
>
> **What you get:**
> - Step sequencer + piano roll + song timeline with loopable clips
> - **202 sounds built in** — 6 studio instruments plus 196 presets across
>   thirteen banks (chiptune `8-bit`, experimental `psy-*`, drums, guitars,
>   neon leads, one-shot FX, mallets & keys)
> - Fully **synthesized in Rust** — no samples, no plugins, works offline
> - Unlimited user-made **JSON instrument packs** (no rebuild needed)
> - Projects save as plain `.cadence` files with crash-recovery autosave
> - Exports **WAV and Standard MIDI** for your DAW
> - Includes the demo song *Afterglow* so you can hear it immediately
>
> **This build:** Windows x64 (tested on Windows 10/11). The app is unsigned,
> so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm — click *More info → Run anyway*.
>
> **Fair deal:** Cadence's source code is free on
> [GitHub](https://github.com/Toom68/cadence). Buy this if you want the
> ready-built, tested Windows binary — or to support a student developer.
>
> Made in Brisbane, Australia.
