# Service agreement — one page, plain English

Between **{{thomas_full_name}}** (trading as "Brissie Sites", Brisbane QLD — "the studio")
and **{{business_name}}** ({{contact_name}} — "the client"). Date: {{date}}.

Because the studio is run by a minor, a parent/guardian co-signs this agreement below.

1. **What we'll do.** The studio has built a one-page website for the client's business
   (the preview at {{preview_url}}) and will finalise it with the client's agreed tweaks,
   launch it, and connect it to the client's domain.

2. **Revisions.** Up to 2 rounds of small changes are included (text, photos, colours,
   hours, services). Bigger changes — new pages, a different design — are quoted
   separately before any work happens.

3. **Price and payment.** A$249 total, payable on delivery: the studio launches the site
   (or delivers the files), sends an invoice, and the client pays by bank transfer or
   PayID within 7 days. Nothing is payable before the client has seen and approved the
   site. No GST applies.

4. **Domain and hosting.** The site is hosted on free static-web hosting — no monthly
   hosting fees from the studio. If the client wants a custom domain, they register it in
   their own name at their own cost (~A$15–25/year); the studio will connect it free.

5. **Ownership.** Once the invoice is paid in full, the website and its files belong to
   the client outright — host it, edit it, move it anywhere. Until payment, the site
   remains the studio's work (the free preview stays free either way).

6. **Care plan.** Optional A$15/month covers small ongoing edits. Either side can cancel
   with an email; the site stays up regardless since the client owns it.

7. **Walking away.** Before payment, either side can end this with an email and nothing
   is owed. No hard feelings — that's the point of building the preview first.

8. **Honest limits.** The studio will do careful, good work but can't promise the site
   will hit particular Google rankings or bring particular revenue — no one honestly can.
