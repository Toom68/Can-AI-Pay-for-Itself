# Cold email — first contact

Three variants of the first email Thomas sends BY HAND (5–10/day max). Every variant is
under 120 words, identifies the sender, states where the address was found, and has a
clear opt-out — all required by the Spam Act 2003.

**Before sending, check `private/optouts.csv` — never contact anyone on it.**

Fill the placeholders per lead:
- `{{business}}` — business name
- `{{greeting_name}}` — owner's first name if known, else "there" (use "Hi there")
- `{{preview_url}}` — link to their built preview
- `{{personal_note}}` — ONE honest sentence about their business (a service they offer,
  a nice review theme, something visible on their Facebook). Never invent a compliment.
- `{{email_source}}` — the exact public place we found the address, e.g.
  "the contact page on your Facebook page" or "your Google Business profile"

Record every send in the outreach log with the source URL.

---

## Variant A — "I already built it" (recommended default)

**Subject:** I built a website for {{business}} — want a look?

Hi {{greeting_name}},

I'm Thomas — I run Brissie Sites, a small student-run web studio in Brisbane. I noticed
{{business}} doesn't have a website, so I went ahead and built one:
{{preview_url}}

{{personal_note}}

It's a free preview — no obligation. If you love it, it's A$249 including setup and
connecting your domain; hosting is free. If not, no worries at all.

I found your email on {{email_source}}. Reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

Thomas
Brissie Sites

## Rules for all variants
- One email per business, sent by hand — never bulk, never automated.
- Keep the opt-out line exactly as written; honour it instantly and permanently.
- Don't claim the preview "took weeks" or that the offer expires — it doesn't.
- If {{greeting_name}} is unknown, "Hi there" is fine; don't guess names.
