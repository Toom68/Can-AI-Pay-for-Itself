# Follow-up

**One follow-up only**, sent 4–5 days after the first email, and ONLY if there was no
reply. Then stop forever — a non-answer is an answer.

Check `private/optouts.csv` first. If they replied at all (even negatively), don't send
this — handle their reply with `reply_playbook.md` instead.

## After the follow-up — hard stop

- No reply within ~7 days → mark the lead "no response" in the outreach log. Done.
- "No thanks" / "unsubscribe" / anything negative → thank them in one short line,
  add the address to `private/optouts.csv` immediately, never contact again.
- Never send a third email. Ever. The business's peace of mind matters more than a sale.
