# Walk-in script — 60 seconds, phone in hand

Optional extra channel: walking into a local shop during a quiet moment with the
preview already loaded on your phone. Works best for businesses that don't answer
email (tradie on-site, busy cafe). Same rules: honest, no pressure, leave happily.

**Do:** pick quiet hours (2–4pm weekdays), dress neatly, lead with the preview not
the pitch, have the studio site open in a second tab, smile and LEAVE if they're busy
or not interested — a friendly "no worries, have a good one" is the whole job sometimes.

**Don't:** go during the rush, read a speech, oversell, or get between staff and customers.

---

## The pitch (~60 seconds)

> Hi — sorry to bother you, I'll be quick. My name's Thomas, I run a small
> student web studio here in Brisbane called Brissie Sites.
>
> I noticed {{business}} doesn't have a website, so I actually **made you one** —
> it's right here, can I show you?
>
> *(hand them the phone / turn it toward them — let them scroll for a few seconds)*
>
> It's already got your services, your hours, and a tap-to-call button. It's free to
> keep looking at — no obligation at all.
>
> If you like it, it's yours for **A$249** — that's everything: setup, connecting your
> own domain, and the hosting's free. It's a one-off, no monthly fees from me.
>
> No pressure either way — want me to send you the link so you can have a proper look
> later? *(get their email/phone or hand a note with the preview URL)*

## If they say yes to more info

> Great — I'll email you the link today. Have a play with it, show anyone you like,
> and just reply if you want it. I'm local, so if it's easier I can even pop back.

## If they hesitate on price

> Totally fair. Most designers charge by the month before you see anything — this is
> the opposite: you saw the finished thing first, and it's A$249 once. Have a think,
> the link's yours.

## If they're not interested

> No worries at all — appreciate your time. If you ever want it, the preview link's
> on the card. Have a good one!

*(Leave a simple hand-written card or a printed slip with the preview URL + contact
email. Nothing fancy.)*

## Notes for Thomas
- Bring a parent or tell one where you're going — basic safety, and some owners warm
  up faster knowing it's a real local family thing.
- Two or three walk-ins per week max; this channel is a bonus, not the plan.
- The preview IS the pitch — get the phone in their hands in the first 15 seconds.
